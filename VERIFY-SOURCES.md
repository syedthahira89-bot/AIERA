## AI weather forecasts (`ai-weather-forecasting`), checked 18 November 2026

Every fact was checked by opening each source page.

- [x] GraphCast: trained on four decades of ERA5 reanalysis data; 0.25 degrees (28 km at the equator), more than a million grid points; 5 surface variables and 6 atmospheric variables at 37 levels; inputs are the weather 6 hours ago and now, predicting 6 hours ahead, rolled forward up to 10 days; under a minute on one TPU v4, against hours on a supercomputer with hundreds of machines for HRES; more accurate than HRES on more than 90% of 1,380 test variables and lead times, and on 99.7% in the troposphere; Hurricane Lee about nine days ahead, against about six days for traditional forecasts; atmospheric rivers and extreme heat; ECMWF live experiment; described as the most accurate 10-day global system ([DeepMind, 14 Nov 2023](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/graphcast-ai-model-for-faster-and-more-accurate-global-weather-forecasting/); [Lam et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12794)).
