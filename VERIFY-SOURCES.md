## Banking and insurance security (`ai-in-banking-and-insurance`)

- [x] FDIC: deposits "are automatically insured to at least $250,000 at each FDIC-insured bank" ([FDIC](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance)). **Corrected:** the article said "up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, for each account ownership category"; it now uses the FDIC's own wording.
- [x] DORA "entered into application on 17 Jan 2025" and covers "banks, insurance companies, investment firms", with resilience testing and reporting of major incidents ([EIOPA](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/digital-operational-resilience-act-dora_en)).
- [x] NIST released Cybersecurity Framework version 2.0 on 26 February 2024 ([NIST news](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2024/02/nist-releases-version-20-landmark-cybersecurity-framework)).
- [x] FS-ISAC is a "recognized platform for threat intelligence sharing … around the world" ([FS-ISAC](https://www.fsisac.com/)).
- [x] PCI DSS is the industry security standard for payment data ([PCI SSC](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/)).
- [x] Passkeys are "phishing-resistant" and "tied to a user's account on a website or application" ([FIDO Alliance](https://fidoalliance.org/passkeys/)).
- [x] The EU AI Act lists AI for creditworthiness and for "risk assessment and pricing … in the case of life and health insurance" as high-risk ([Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)).
- **Removed:** the $50 US limit on unauthorised credit card charges, and the debit card reporting rule. The FTC website blocks automated readers, and the linked page could not be confirmed.
