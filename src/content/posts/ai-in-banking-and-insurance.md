## How widely AI is used

In a survey of UK financial firms published in November 2024, the Bank of England and the Financial Conduct Authority found that **75%** of firms were already using AI. Insurers had the highest share, at **95%**, with international banks just behind at **94%**.

## Three habits to start today

1. **Turn on passkeys or two-step sign-in** with your bank, if it offers them.
2. **Switch on payment alerts**, so you notice anything unusual quickly.
3. **Ask why.** If a lending or insurance decision doesn't go your way, ask for the main reasons.
