Have you ever wondered how people decide which AI model is "best"? Often, they check a **leaderboard**, and many of the most popular ones live on **Hugging Face**.

## What is Hugging Face?

Hugging Face is a website where people share AI models, datasets and demos, a bit like a friendly library for AI. Anyone can browse it, try models in the browser, and see how they compare.

## What does a leaderboard show?

A leaderboard is a scoreboard. Models take the same set of tests, called **benchmarks**, and get ranked by their scores. Some leaderboards test general knowledge or reasoning; others focus on maths, coding or a particular language. One of the best known was the **Open LLM Leaderboard**, which compared thousands of openly shared language models side by side.

## How to read one wisely

- **Check what's being tested.** A top score in maths says little about writing a friendly email.
- **Look at the size of the gap.** Models a fraction of a point apart are usually equally good.
- **Try it yourself.** The best model is the one that works well for *your* task.

> Curious? Search for "leaderboard" on [Hugging Face Spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces?search=leaderboard) and see how many different scoreboards there are.
